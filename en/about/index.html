About the Forum

The Alexander Forum für Reflexion & Resilienz (Alexander Forum for Reflection & Resilience) was founded in 2025 by Dorian Rammer in Vienna. It is an independent, not-for-profit think & do tank that understands societal crises and personal burdens as interconnected phenomena.

Where the Forum stands

The Forum is young. It is currently led by its founder, an advisory board is being set up, and working drafts are open for review. In the long run it is meant to grow into an institute for analysis, reflection and practical resilience work, open to cooperation, contributions and shared development.

Legally, the Forum is currently a project of its founder; an association is in the process of being founded. Details in the imprint.

Our approach

We live in a time of multiple overloads: climate crisis, geopolitical tensions, digital disorientation, social fragmentation. At the same time, psychological stress, loneliness and exhaustion are increasing. These two levels, the societal and the personal, are intertwined: those under pressure can contribute less to the community, and unresolved societal tensions weaken every individual.

We therefore understand resilience not as individual “endurance” but as a shared design task. It is about creating conditions under which people and communities do not merely survive but can thrive.

How we work

We focus on intellectual depth, written precision and interdisciplinary synthesis. Our work connects psychology, political science, sociology, medicine, history and philosophy. We acknowledge uncertainty openly, work with sources and look for perspectives that get lost in daily debate.

We favour reflection over information overload, and dialogue that tolerates contradiction and takes different perspectives seriously. Our work is meant to stay practical: in analyses and working papers, and in future also in briefings and workshops that enable exchange and strengthen resilience as a shared attitude.

Principles

  • Context before reflex: we ask not only what happens, but how people and systems reach their breaking points.
  • Responsibility without dehumanisation: explanations do not replace boundaries, but they improve prevention.
  • Proportionality: we weigh problem, measure and unintended side effects against each other.
  • Transparency: we mark uncertainty instead of glossing over it.
  • Interdisciplinarity: psychology, politics, medicine and the social sciences are read together on purpose.

Leadership

Dorian Rammer

Founder and director

Born in 1996 and based in Vienna. Background in politics, emergency medical services and communications, with three years of formal training in early childhood education. Writer and public commentator on mental health, addiction and contemporary societal crises, in essays, articles and short videos; featured on the Austrian broadcaster ORF’s programme Thema in 2025 (reach approx. 550,000).

Dorian works at the intersection of politics, psychosocial practice and societal analysis. His perspective combines lived crisis experience with empirical evidence, not as anecdote but as an analytical entry point into systemic dynamics: How does resilience develop? What do people need to move through crises? Where do systems fail, and how can they be strengthened?

Focus areas: interpersonal dynamics, trauma, addiction, identity, civic courage and social cohesion.
