Formats

Working papers
Drafts in progress, open for professional, institutional or public review. Version numbers show the current state; feedback goes into the next version.
Foundation dossiers
Detailed analyses with sources, as web pages and PDF.
Policy briefs
Planned: 3,000 to 5,000 words, concise and focused on implementation.
Numbering
Each series is counted per year, for example “AF Working Paper 2026/1” or “AF Dossier 2025/2”.
